REFERENCE ROOM — VISUAL DIRECTION STARTER

Use these as building blocks in any AI image prompt. Combine a point of view,
place, light, material, and mood to make your image more specific.

1. POINT OF VIEW
"observed from the doorway, as if arriving late"
"close enough to notice the fingerprint on the glass"
"a low angle that makes an ordinary object feel important"

2. PLACE
"a sun-warmed laundromat just before closing"
"a quiet apartment kitchen after a long conversation"
"a narrow corner store with handmade signs and fluorescent light"

3. LIGHT
"soft morning light interrupted by window blinds"
"a single warm lamp against blue evening shadows"
"flash photography, slightly imperfect and alive"

4. TEXTURE
"creased linen, brushed metal, paper edges, and worn leather"
"light grain, imperfect focus, archival print feeling"
"matte surfaces with one small high-shine detail"

5. MOOD
"private but not lonely"
"quiet confidence, no performance"
"familiar, collected, and slightly unfinished"

PROMPT FORMULA
[subject] + [point of view] + [place] + [light] + [texture] + [mood]

Example: A pair of red shoes on a kitchen chair, observed from the doorway, in a quiet apartment kitchen after a long conversation, a single warm lamp against blue evening shadows, creased linen and matte wood, private but not lonely.
